📚 Trading Academy

Learn important trading concepts.

1. Market Structure

Understand Higher Highs (HH), Higher Lows (HL), Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).

2. Break of Structure (BOS)

BOS occurs when price breaks an important previous swing level, potentially showing trend continuation.

3. Change of Character (CHoCH)

CHoCH is a possible early signal that market structure is changing direction.

4. Liquidity

Learn about buy-side liquidity and sell-side liquidity around important price levels.

5. Order Blocks

Study price areas traders use to identify possible institutional activity.
